# Access matrix

_Generated by `npm run docs:access` from `src/lib/permissions/`. Do not edit by hand._

## How access is decided

```
effective = (general ∪ roles ∪ grants) ∩ ceiling(account type) − locks
```

- **BFT MENA Full access** (`admin`) passes every check and holds no roles.
- A person can hold **several roles**; their permissions add up. A person holding none gets the default role for their account type:
  - `staff` → **BFT MENA Partial**
  - `studio` → **Gym / Studio**
  - `competitor` → **Athlete**
- Per person, any permission can be **granted** or **locked**. A lock always wins.
- **Policies**: _everyone_ — always on, even while waiting for approval; _role_ — given by roles and grants; _BFT MENA only_ — never reaches a studio, organiser or athlete account; _Full access only_ — never given to anyone.
- **Anti-escalation**: you can only give what you hold (Full access exempt); nobody changes their own access; a studio gives only roles marked "studios can give", only to its own people.

## Who may give each role

| Role | Given by | Meant for | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BFT MENA Partial | BFT MENA | staff | BFT MENA staff with chosen screens only. Starts as view-only everywhere. |
| Gym / Studio | BFT MENA | studio | Runs its own gym: approves its people, registers and pairs its athletes, places its teams in waves. No score entry. |
| Organiser | BFT MENA and studios | organiser, studio, staff | Sees everything in a competition and runs the floor: waves, wave control, zone teams. Does not edit teams or enter scores. |
| Judge | BFT MENA and studios | organiser, studio, staff | Enters scores for the zone and station they are placed on. Nothing else. |
| Volunteer | BFT MENA | organiser | Sees the wave schedule and the live board. |
| Coach | BFT MENA | organiser | Sees the wave schedule, results and the live board. |
| Athlete | BFT MENA and studios | competitor | Their own profile, partner, team and wave. |
